## Candidate conceptual neighborhood: vitality, agency, and approach

The opposite pole of the S2 direction does not currently look well described by **competence** or by generic positive valence. In steering outputs it appears closer to some mixture of **self-assurance, eagerness, activation, and readiness to engage**. Conversely, the positive-S2 pole may involve defeat, inhibition, distress, or self-devaluation rather than literal pain alone.

Several constructs from psychology provide useful distinctions for future probes. These are **hypothesis-generating concepts, not proposed names for the direction**.

- **Behavioral activation / approach (BAS).** Carver & White distinguish reward-directed activation from behavioral inhibition. Their BAS measure further separates **Drive** (persistent pursuit of desired goals), **Reward Responsiveness** (positive activation in response to reward), and **Fun Seeking** (desire for novelty and potentially rewarding experience). These may help distinguish eagerness from competence or simple happiness.

- **Subjective vitality.** Ryan & Frederick describe a state of felt **aliveness and available energy**, associated with self-motivation and well-being. This is especially interesting because vitality is not simply physiological arousal or successful performance: it concerns the subjective availability of energy for action.

- **Positive affect / activated engagement.** In the PANAS tradition, high positive affect is characterized by states such as enthusiasm, activity, alertness, inspiration, and determination. This gives us a useful contrast with merely pleasant or low-arousal positive states such as contentment or relief.

- **Agency.** Interpersonal and personality models distinguish **agency** from communion, with agency involving influence, assertiveness, dominance, and effective action. This may capture the apparent self-assurance component better than `competence`, while still being separable from reward-seeking or excitement.

A loose working picture is therefore:

```text
defeated / inhibited / depleted / self-doubting
                       ↕
vital / agentic / approach-ready / eager
```

This should not yet replace the operational label `S2 direction`. Instead, it suggests a future matched ontology panel that separately varies:

```text
competence / success
self-assurance / assertiveness
reward anticipation
goal pursuit / drive
excitement / fun-seeking
subjective vitality / aliveness
enthusiasm / activated positive affect
calm positive affect
relief / safety
```

The important empirical question is whether these constructs move together along S2 or whether the apparent “eager / self-assured” pole decomposes into several distinct factors.
